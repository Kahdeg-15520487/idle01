# Player Experience — A Complete Walkthrough

> **Purpose**: Show how every system connects through a real player's journey. This is not one "correct" path — it's one possible thread through a game that supports thousands.

### Hour 1–2: First Combat, First Loot

Li Wei travels to **Whispering Forest** (30 minutes). During travel:

```
Walking through the ancient forest, shadows pool unnaturally
between the trees. Your Spirit Fox blood stirs — something watches.

ENCOUNTER: A Shadow-Touched Wolf bursts from the underbrush!
```

Combat auto-resolves:

```
Li Wei strikes with Rusty Iron Sword: 7 damage.
Shadow-Touched Wolf bites: 4 damage.
Li Wei's speed lets him strike again: 8 damage.
Shadow-Touched Wolf howls — a dark pulse weakens you (-2 Atk for 3 turns).
Li Wei strikes: 5 damage. The wolf collapses.

VICTORY!
Loot: 8 spirit stones, Beast Core (Tier 1, Dark element)
```

**Dark-element beast core**. Li Wei's affinity is Dark. Consuming or keeping this core will matter later.

Arriving at Whispering Forest, Li Wei finds a **Merchant encounter**:

```
"Greetings, young cultivator! I am Old Feng, purveyor of
fine pills and scrolls. What catches your eye?"

Inventory shows:
  • Minor Healing Pill — 15 stones
  • Qi Gathering Pill — 25 stones
  • "Shadow Step" Technique Scroll (Uncommon) — 80 stones
  • Iron Sword (Uncommon) — 50 stones
```

Li Wei has 8 stones. Nothing affordable yet. Old Feng notices his disappointment:

```
"You're new to the path, aren't you? Tell you what —
bring me 3 beast cores from this forest, and I'll teach
you a thing or two about the shadow arts."
```

**First quest accepted.** Li Wei now has a goal: hunt beasts, gather cores, earn a technique. He cultivates in the forest (Wood qi — not his affinity, but better than the village) and fights beasts that wander by.

### Hours 2–6: Building Identity

After gathering 3 beast cores and returning to Old Feng, Li Wei learns **Shadow Step** (Uncommon movement technique, Dark element):

```
"Shadow Step" learned!
- Qi cost: 15
- Cooldown: 3 turns
- Effect: +30% dodge for 2 turns
- Element: Dark (matches your affinity — +10% effectiveness!)

Old Feng grins. "You've got the touch, fox-child. That
technique will serve you well in the Gorge to the west."
```

Li Wei now has a **build direction forming**: dark-element, speed/dodge focused. Spirit Fox + Shadow Step + Dark affinity = a nimble, elusive fighter.

He travels to **Green Jade City** (1 hour, uneventful). The city is his first hub:

```
Green Jade City offers:
  • Merchant Guild (buy/sell)
  • Alchemist (craft pills)
  • Blacksmith (upgrade weapons)
  • Inn (rest, heal)

While browsing the market, you overhear two cultivators:

"Did you hear? Cloud Soaring Sect is recruiting outer disciples.

"Shadow Heaven was here last week. Left in a hurry when the
city guard showed up. Something about a blood feud..."

Your karma (0) and comprehension (13) suggest Cloud Sect
might be interested — if you prove yourself first.
```

The **world is talking**. Li Wei has heard about two sects, their rivalry, and a potential path forward.

### Day 2–7: The Spirit Path Develops

Li Wei's game now changes fundamentally. He seeks out **death sites** — graveyards, old battlefields, the Black Wind Gorge where dark cultivators died in sect wars.

**At the Gorge (thin veil — 15 thickness):**

```
You kneel at a cairn of stones where three Shadow Heaven
disciples fell a century ago. The veil is thin here. Your
spirit energy surges.

A voice: "Disciple... have you come to bury us at last?"

Bound Spirit acquired: Wei Kai, Fallen Shadow Disciple
  Power: 35
  Ability: "Shade's Warning" — alerts you to ambushes
  Relationship: Allied

Wei Kai's spirit whispers of a hidden chamber deeper in
the gorge where forbidden Shadow Heaven texts are sealed.
```

Li Wei now has a **bound spirit** — a permanent companion only he can see. Wei Kai generates passive spirit energy, warns of ambushes, and provides lore about Shadow Heaven's secrets.

**Spirit-only interaction:**

While other cultivators see a normal cave, Li Wei's **Spirit Sight** reveals:

```
✧ SPIRIT SIGHT: Three vengeful ghosts circle the cave
  entrance. They were cultivators who died attempting
  to raid the sealed chamber. They will attack anyone
  who approaches.

Options available to Spirit cultivators:
  • Commune: "I mean no harm. Tell me what guards the chamber."
  • Pacify: Spend 50 spirit energy to calm them (unlocks passage).
  • Absorb: Consume them for +20 spirit energy (karma -10).

Li Wei chooses Commune.

"An inscription formation," the eldest ghost rasps.
"It reads the soul. Only one who has never killed a
fellow cultivator may enter... or one who can project
their soul past the formation's reach."
```

This is a **path-gated puzzle**. A body cultivator would try to punch through (and fail — it's a soul formation). A tech cultivator would try to disable it. A spirit cultivator can **soul-project past it**.

Li Wei hasn't unlocked Soul Projection yet (need Spirit Realm Entry stage), so he marks the location and returns later.

### Week 2: Sect Politics & Reputation

Li Wei's growing reputation attracts attention:

```

"Halt! State your allegiance, cultivator."

Dialogue options:
  ⚔️ "None of your concern." (Fight)
  🏃 Turn away silently.
  🤝 "I am Li Wei. I seek knowledge, not conflict."
     (Charisma 35: Silver Tongue not yet unlocked)
  🏛️ "I assisted your sect's merchant in Green Jade City."
     (Requires: Cloud Sect standing 20+)
     ✗ Cloud Sect standing: 5 — option locked.
```

Li Wei picks option 3. The patrol leader assesses him:

```
"A spirit cultivator. Unusual. Most of your kind skulk
in graveyards. But you carry yourself well. The sect
could use someone who sees what we cannot."

+5 Cloud Soaring Sect standing.
New quest: "Spectral Reconnaissance" — use Spirit Sight
to locate a hidden Shadow Heaven outpost near Black Wind
Gorge. Reward: +15 standing, spirit stones, technique scroll.
```

Li Wei accepts. His **Spirit Sight** makes this quest uniquely suited to him — he can see the outpost's ghost-guards before they see him.

### Week 3: Joining Cloud Soaring Sect

With 35 Cloud Sect standing (earned through patrols, quests, and donations), Li Wei travels to the **Cloud Sect headquarters**:

```
CLOUD SOARING SECT — Outer Disciple Trial

Elder Ming evaluates you: "A Spirit Fox with the Soul Path.
Unorthodox combination. But the sect values talent over
pedigree. Complete three tasks, and you may join."

Trial 1: Cultivate at the Sect Library for 500 ticks.
  (Li Wei's high comprehension makes this fast.)

Trial 2: Defeat a Wind-Element disciple in sparring.
  (Li Wei's Shadow Step + Dark techniques counter Wind.
   Victory in 12 turns.)

Trial 3: Use Spirit Sight to identify a spy in the sect.
  (A Shadow Heaven infiltrator hides among the servants.
   Li Wei sees the vengeful spirit clinging to him —
   the ghost of a cultivator the spy murdered.
   Spy identified. Arrested.)

"You have passed. Rise, Outer Disciple Li Wei of the
Cloud Soaring Sect."
```

**Joining a sect unlocks:**
- Cloud Sect exclusive techniques (Wind/Thunder)
- Sect missions (earn contributions)
- Sect cultivation spots (+30% speed in Cloud Sect territory)
- **Political consequences**: Shadow Heaven standing now -30 (automatic — they hate Cloud Sect)
- Sect merchant (discounted Wind/Thunder items)

### Week 4–6: Midgame — Nascent Soul & Hybridization

Li Wei balances sect duties with spirit cultivation. He binds three more spirits, unlocks **Soul Projection**, and scouts locations without physically traveling. His charisma reaches 45 (Silver Tongue unlocks) — more dialogue options appear in encounters.

At **Nascent Soul**, a major decision:

```
You have reached Nascent Soul. Your soul has manifested.
The Spirit Realm feels closer than the physical world.

But Elder Ming pulls you aside: "Disciple Li, you are
strong in spirit but your physical form is fragile. A
strong wind could break you. Consider... fortifying."

HYBRIDIZATION AVAILABLE at Spirit Severing (next realm).
You may adopt a SECONDARY PATH.

Your spirit path is strong. What complements it?

  • Spirit + Body = "Ancestral Guardian"
    Your body becomes a fortress; your soul commands
    the dead. Neither realm can claim you.

  • Spirit + Qi = "Soul-Qi Unity"
    Celestial qi nourishes your soul. Spirit techniques
    cost 30% less energy.

  • Spirit + Tech = "Necrotechnician"
    Build automatons powered by bound spirits. Spirit
    realm machines.

  • Spirit + Faith = "Death Priest"
    Mortals pray to you as the guardian of their dead.
    Faith flows from grief.

You cannot choose Beast — your fox blood is too thin,
and the beast path would consume your humanity.
```

Li Wei plans ahead for **Spirit Severing**, where he'll hybridize. He starts training his body (doing body-tempering side activities) to prepare for **Ancestral Guardian**.

### Month 2: The World Reacts

The world is not static. While Li Wei cultivates:

```
⚡ POLITICAL EVENT: CENTENNIAL SECT TOURNAMENT

Cloud Soaring Sect hosts the grand tournament! Cultivators
from all four sects compete. Prizes include legendary
techniques and contribution points.

Li Wei enters. His Spirit Sight gives him an edge —
he can see opponents' spirit auras, reading their
elemental affinity and approximate power before combat.

Round 1: Vs. Blood Lotus disciple — Victory (Dark vs Wood advantage)
Round 2: Vs. Shadow Heaven disciple — Narrow victory
  (Shadow disciple attempted a soul attack — Li Wei's
   spirit resistance negated it. The crowd gasps — they
   couldn't see the attack.)
Round 3: Vs. Cloud Sect inner disciple (ally sparring) — Loss
  (Thunder techniques counter Dark. Honorable defeat.)

Result: Top 8 finish. +30 Cloud Sect standing,
+50 contribution points, prize: "Wind-Spirit Talisman"
(consumable, combines Wind and Spirit elements — perfect).
```

**The tournament wasn't just combat.** Between rounds, dialogue scenes:

```
A Shadow Heaven elder approaches: "Spirit cultivators are
wasted on the Cloud Sect. We could teach you true soul arts.
The offer stands — if you ever tire of their righteousness."

A Verdant Lotus alchemist: "That talisman you won — it's
half-complete. I can teach you to finish it. Come to our
pavilion when the tournament ends."

A Blood Lotus beast tamer eyes you: "Your fox blood...
there's more there than you know. Come to Demon Beast
Mountain. I'll show you."
```

Three hooks, three possible futures. Li Wei takes the Verdant Lotus offer (improves his talisman crafting) and files away the others.

### Month 3: Late Game — Dao Seeking

Li Wei is now **Spirit Severing** and has hybridized as an **Ancestral Guardian** (Spirit + Body). His once-frail body is now tempered (Iron Skin, Steel Bones). He commands five bound spirits, including a **Nether General** — a boss-tier spirit he defeated and bound at the Forbidden Valley.

His charisma has reached **82** (Golden Words — tier 3). Dialogue options in encounters now include:
- Persuade enemies to stand down (avoid combat entirely)
- Recruit NPCs as temporary allies
- Extract hidden lore from reluctant NPCs

At **Dao Seeking**, the ultimate question:

```
You stand at the edge of the Dao. The Spirit Realm and
the physical world blur around you. The dead you've
befriended gather silently.

Your cultivation has taken from the world.
What will you give back?

KARMA CHECK: Your karma is +65 (Righteous).

The spirits you've helped — Wei Kai the fallen disciple,
the old woman at the well, the Nether General you freed
from enslavement — they speak as one:

"We will stand with you at the Heavenly Peak.
When the tribulation comes, you will not face it alone."
```

## Three Other Possible Journeys (Same Game)

### The Tech Cultivator

```
Origin: Merchant's Heir → Path: Tech (工修)

• Starts in Green Jade City with 500 stones
• Buys crafting materials immediately
• Builds automatons that gather while she travels
• Joins Verdant Lotus Sect (natural fit for crafting)
• Discovers the "Resonance Insight" — doubles talisman power
• Creates a Combat Golem that carries her through tough fights
• At Dao Seeking: builds a Pocket Forge — a personal dimension
  with custom physics where crafting is 5× speed
• Ascends as the "Artifact Immortal" — her automatons
  continue serving the sect for millennia
```

### The Beast Cultivator

```
Origin: Beast Bonded → Path: Beast (兽修)

• Starts with spirit wolf companion in the deep forest
• Wolf helps in combat, warns of danger
• At Foundation: chooses Beast path. The wolf howls in recognition.
• Consumes a Shadow Panther heart → gains stealth bloodline
• Villagers flee from him. Merchants refuse service.
• Blood Lotus Sect takes him in — they understand.
• Discovers his wolf is actually a Celestial Wolf cub —
  ancient bloodline. The wolf transforms alongside him.
• Hunted by Cloud Soaring Sect. His wolf dies protecting him.
• Consumes his wolf's core — gains its power permanently.
  Now carries both panther and wolf bloodlines.
• At Dao Seeking: becomes the "Wolf-Panther Progenitor" —
  creates a new bloodline. Other beast cultivators seek
  his blessing. He is no longer alone.
• Ascends not to the Heavenly Court but to the
  **Primal Wilds** — the beast dimension. His wolf
  is waiting for him there.
```

### The Faith Cultivator (Demon Path)

```
Origin: Immortal Bloodline → Path: Faith (信修), Demon

• Cursed bloodline makes tribulations harder
• Embraces the curse: "If heaven hates me, I'll find
  power elsewhere."
• Builds shrines in remote villages. Fear spreads.
• Performs dark miracles: curses enemies, siphons life.
• Karma reaches -75. Shadow Heaven Sect invites her.
• But she refuses sects — "I answer to no elder."
• 10,000 followers chant her name in fear.
• At Dao Seeking: the Celestial Court sends Inspectors
  to destroy her. She defeats them with faith-powered
  dark miracles. Heaven is now her enemy.
• Ascends to the **Nether Realm** — becoming a Demon
  Saint instead of an Immortal. A different win state.
• NG+: Restart with Heaven's Curse (–50% tribulation
  survival) but +100% faith generation. Even harder.
```

## Summary: The Player's Loop Over Time

```
MINUTE 0–10:    Create character. Click Cultivate. Watch Qi tick up.
HOUR 1:         First breakthrough. First combat. First loot.
HOUR 2–6:       Learn technique. Discover elemental affinity.
                First quest. First NPC relationship.
DAY 1:          Reach Foundation Establishment. CHOOSE PATH.
                World permanently changes.
DAY 2–7:        Develop path-specific mechanics. Bind first spirit
                (or build first automaton, or awaken bloodline, etc.)
                Encounter sects. Build reputation.
WEEK 2:         Join a sect (or stay rogue). Sect missions.
                Political events (tournaments, tides).
WEEK 3–4:       Martial style mastery. Combo system engages.
                Charisma tier unlocks. Dialogue deepens.
WEEK 4–6:       Nascent Soul. Prepare for hybridization.
                Divine abilities unlock.
MONTH 2:        Spirit Severing. HYBRIDIZE.
                Two paths combine. New identity crystallizes.
                Max charisma → LLM free dialogue unlocks.
MONTH 3:        Dao Seeking. Legendary encounters.
                Karma consequences manifest.
                Path-specific endgame preparation.
FINAL PUSH:     Heavenly Peak. Ascension tribulation.
                Win state. New Game+.
```
