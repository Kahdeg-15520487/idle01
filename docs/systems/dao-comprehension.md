# Dao Comprehension — The Knowledge Gate

> **Role**: The second axis of progression. Shén (energy) is the fuel. Dao comprehension is the **understanding** — you can have all the power in the world, but without insight into the fundamental laws, you cannot break through to higher tiers.
>
> **Principle**: Beyond accumulating energy, the player must **solve** — riddles, visions, trials, and story choices that reveal the nature of reality.

---

## Comprehension Levels

Each Dao has five levels of understanding. You don't "level up" a Dao by spending points — you **earn insight** by solving puzzles, passing trials, and making choices that demonstrate understanding.

| Level | Name | What It Means | How You Get It |
|-------|------|---------------|----------------|
| **0** | Unaware | You don't know this Dao exists. | Default. |
| **1** | Glimpsed (瞥) | You've seen a shadow of the truth. | A story event reveals it. A teacher mentions it. You witness something inexplicable. |
| **2** | Understood (悟) | You grasp the principle. | Solve a Dao puzzle. Pass a trial. Make the right choice in a Dao-testing event. |
| **3** | Internalized (化) | The Dao is part of your thinking. | Apply the Dao successfully in multiple situations. Combine with another Dao. |
| **4** | Embodied (体) | You ARE the Dao in microcosm. | Survive a Dao tribulation. Create something that demonstrates the Dao. Change the world with it. |
| **5** | Transcended (超) | You see beyond the Dao itself. | Reserved for Tier 7+. You understand the Dao so completely you can break its rules. |

## Tier Breakthrough Requirements

Energy is not enough. At higher tiers, the player must have comprehended specific Daos:

| Tier | Shén Required | Dao Requirement |
|------|--------------|-----------------|
| **0 → 1** | 10 S | None. Pure instinct. |
| **1 → 2** | 1 kS | None. Basic cultivation. |
| **2 → 3** | 100 kS | **One Dao at Glimpsed (1).** Your path's primary Dao is revealed to you. |
| **3 → 4** | 10 MS | **Primary Dao at Understood (2).** You truly grasp your path's foundation. |
| **4 → 5** | 1 GS | **Primary Dao at Internalized (3) + one other Dao at Glimpsed (1).** You see connections. |
| **5 → 6** | 100 GS | **Primary Dao at Embodied (4) + secondary Dao at Understood (2) + third Dao at Glimpsed (1).** You are a master of your truth. |
| **6 → 7** | 10 TS | **Primary Dao at Transcended (5) + secondary Dao at Internalized (3) + third Dao at Understood (2).** You see beyond the Dao itself. |

## How Dao Comprehension Is Earned — The Puzzles

### 1. Story Events With Hidden Dao Tests

Some events contain a Dao test disguised as a normal choice. The player may not even know they're being tested:

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  The Burning Village                                     ║
║                                                          ║
║  A village is on fire. Screams fill the night. You can    ║
║  save the people — or let the fire burn and absorb the    ║
║  flames' qi.                                             ║
║                                                          ║
║  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐    ║
║  │ 🔥 "Absorb the flames. Their qi is purer than any │    ║
║  │    I've felt."                                    │    ║
║  │    +Qi. The village burns. -Karma.                │    ║
║  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘    ║
║  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐    ║
║  │ 💧 "Use water techniques to fight the fire."       │    ║
║  │    (Requires: Water Dao glimpsed)                 │    ║
║  │    Village saved. +Karma.                         │    ║
║  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘    ║
║  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐    ║
║  │ 🔥💧 "Fire is not destruction — it is              │    ║
║  │    transformation. Let it cleanse, then rebuild."  │    ║
║  │    (Requires: Fire Dao glimpsed, Comprehension 20)│    ║
║  │    Village partially saved. Fire Dao advances.    │    ║
║  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘    ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

The third choice advances Fire Dao comprehension. The player recognizes fire's duality — destroyer AND renewer. This is not spelled out as "pick this to level up Fire Dao." The player must **understand** why that choice is different.

### 2. Meditation Visions

At certain locations, the player can trigger a meditation event. A vision plays out — a short narrative scene — and the player must interpret it:

```
╔══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║  MEDITATION — The Ancient Kiln                           ║
║                                                          ║
║  You meditate in the ruins of an ancient kiln where      ║
║  immortal smiths once forged reality itself.              ║
║                                                          ║
║  A vision: A smith hammers a blade. Each strike makes    ║
║  it stronger. But with the hundredth strike, the blade   ║
║  shatters. The smith weeps — not for the blade, but      ║
║  because he did not know when to stop.                    ║
║                                                          ║
║  What truth does the vision teach?                       ║
║                                                          ║
║  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐    ║
║  │ "Perfection requires endless refinement."         │    ║
║  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘    ║
║  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐    ║
║  │ "Knowing when to stop is part of mastery."        │    ║
║  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘    ║
║  ┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────┐    ║
║  │ "The metal was flawed from the beginning."        │    ║
║  └──────────────────────────────────────────────────┘    ║
╚══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝
```

- Choice 1: Wrong. The smith refined endlessly and the blade broke.
- Choice 2: **Correct.** The Metal Dao truth — precision includes knowing limits. Metal Dao advances.
- Choice 3: Wrong. The vision wasn't about the material's quality.

### 3. Dao Trials (Location-Based)

Specific locations offer Dao trials — gauntlets that test understanding:

| Dao | Trial Location | Trial |
|-----|---------------|-------|
| **Fire** | Volcanic Caldera | Walk through a chamber of escalating flame. Survive without resisting — let the fire pass THROUGH you. |
| **Water** | Abyssal Trench | Sink to the deepest point. The pressure will kill you unless you flow WITH it. |
| **Wood** | Ancient Ironwood Forest | The trees attack. Fight them and lose. Befriend them and they teach you. |
| **Metal** | Thunder Peak | Stand on the peak during a storm. The lightning will strike you. If you conduct it to the earth without flinching, you understand Metal. |
| **Earth** | Gravity Cave | The gravity increases every step. You cannot turn back. You reach the center or you are crushed. |
| **Time/Light** | Heavenly Peak (dawn) | Watch the sunrise from the peak without blinking, eating, or moving for 1,000 ticks. The light will show you eternity. |
| **Space/Dark** | Black Wind Gorge (new moon) | Navigate the gorge in absolute darkness. No torches. No qi sight. Trust the void. |
| **Soul/Life/Death** | Forbidden Valley | Die. (A spirit will kill you temporarily.) Experience death. Return. |
| **Faith/Karma** | Any city with 10,000+ mortals | Live among them for 5,000 ticks without using cultivation. Be human. |

### 4. Cross-Dao Insights

Combining two Daos can unlock insights neither alone provides:

| Dao Pair | Combined Insight | How To Discover |
|----------|-----------------|-----------------|
| Fire + Water | "Steam carries more force than either alone." | Witness a geyser. |
| Wood + Earth | "The tallest tree feeds on the deepest root." | Meditate at an ancient tree. |
| Metal + Time | "Rust is not decay — it is metal returning to the earth's flow." | Find a rusted legendary weapon. |
| Space + Soul | "The soul is not IN the body. The body is a point the soul visits." | Soul-project while physically moving. |
| Light + Dark | "Light creates shadows. Shadows prove light. Neither exists without the other." | Watch an eclipse. |
| Death + Karma | "What you do echoes in what you become after death." | Die and be resurrected. |

## Path-Aligned Daos

Each cultivation path has natural affinities with certain Daos. The path's primary Dao is the easiest to advance — but the player can comprehend any Dao.

| Path | Primary Dao | Secondary Daos | Hardest Dao |
|------|-----------|---------------|-------------|
| **Qi** | Varies by element affinity | Time/Light, Space/Dark | Faith/Karma (qi is impersonal) |
| **Body** | Earth | Metal, Fire | Space/Dark (body is physical) |
| **Spirit** | Soul/Life/Death | Space/Dark, Water | Fire (spirit fears flame) |
| **Tech** | Metal | Time/Light, Earth | Faith/Karma (tech trusts rules, not belief) |
| **Faith** | Faith/Karma | Soul/Life/Death, Light | Space/Dark (faith seeks light) |
| **Beast** | Wood (growth, wild) | Fire, Earth | Time/Light (beasts live in the now) |

## API

```typescript
// ─── Dao State ──────────────────────────────

interface DaoState {
  readonly id: DaoId;
  readonly level: 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5;
  readonly insights: string[];       // Text descriptions of player's understanding
  readonly trialHistory: DaoTrialRecord[];
}

type DaoId = 'fire' | 'water' | 'wood' | 'metal' | 'earth'
           | 'time_light' | 'space_dark' | 'soul_life_death' | 'faith_karma';

interface DaoTrialRecord {
  readonly trialId: string;
  readonly passed: boolean;
  readonly insightGained: string;
  readonly tick: number;
}

// ─── Dao Progression ────────────────────────

function getDaoLevel(player: PlayerState, dao: DaoId): number;
function canAdvanceDao(player: PlayerState, dao: DaoId): boolean;
function advanceDao(state: GameState, dao: DaoId, insight: string): GameState;

// ─── Breakthrough Gate ──────────────────────

function getDaoRequirement(tier: number): DaoRequirement[];
function meetsDaoRequirement(player: PlayerState, tier: number): boolean;
function getMissingDaos(player: PlayerState, tier: number): DaoRequirement[];

// ─── Trials & Puzzles ───────────────────────

function getAvailableTrials(player: PlayerState, locationId: string): DaoTrial[];
function attemptTrial(state: GameState, trialId: string, playerAnswer?: string): ActionResult<GameState>;
function evaluateDaoPuzzle(puzzle: DaoPuzzle, playerChoice: string): PuzzleResult;
function checkCrossDaoInsight(player: PlayerState, dao1: DaoId, dao2: DaoId): Insight | null;
```
