# Energy Tier System — Universal Power Comparison

> **Role**: A physics-based measuring stick. Every cultivator, regardless of path, has a total energy capacity measured in joules. This lets you compare a Body cultivator's "Jade Body" to a Qi cultivator's "Nascent Soul" — same tier, different expression.
>
> **Does NOT replace**: Realm names. Each path keeps its own progression with its own flavor. Energy tiers are the underlying number.

---

## The Universal Scale

Energy is energy. Qi, kinetic force, spirit resonance, faith — all of it is joules. The tier system says: *if you can command X joules, you are Tier N, regardless of how you got there.*

| Energy Tier | Energy Required | Qi Path Realm | Body Path Stage | Spirit Path Stage | Tech Path Stage | Faith Path Stage | Beast Path Stage |
|-------------|----------------|---------------|-----------------|-------------------|-----------------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **0** | 10² J | Mortal | Untempered | Unawakened | Apprentice | Acolyte | Blood Dormant |
| **1** | 10³ J | Qi Condensation L1–3 | Iron Skin | Soul Awakening | Journeyman | Preacher | Blood Awakening |
| **2** | 10⁴ J | Qi Condensation L4–9 | Steel Bones | Spirit Sense | Master Artificer | Priest | Beast Mark |
| **3** | 10⁵ J | Foundation Establishment | Jade Body | Soul Projection | Grandmaster | High Priest | Partial Transformation |
| **4** | 10⁶ J | Core Formation | Diamond Meridians | Spirit Realm Entry | Sage of Mechanisms | Saint / Demon Saint | Full Transformation |
| **5** | 10⁷ J | Nascent Soul | Golden Body | Soul Dominion | Dao Architect | Living Buddha / Demon King | Ancient Blood |
| **6** | 10⁸ J | Spirit Severing | Indestructible Flesh | Reincarnation Memory | World Smith | Ascended Deity | Primordial Beast |
| **7** | 10⁹ J | Dao Seeking | Realm-Shattering Fist | Eternal Soul | — | — | Beast Progenitor |

A **Tier 4 Body cultivator** (Diamond Meridians, ~1 MJ) and a **Tier 4 Qi cultivator** (Core Formation, ~1 MJ) command roughly equal total energy. One expresses it as physical force. The other as qi techniques. They are peers. They can fight evenly. The world treats them as the same rank.

## The Exponential Curve

Each tier demands **10× more energy** than the last.

| Tier | Energy | Real-World Equivalent | What You Can Do |
|------|--------|----------------------|-----------------|
| 0 | 10² J (100 J) | A human punch | Baseline. No cultivation. |
| 1 | 10³ J (1 kJ) | A sword swing | Sense qi. First breakthrough. |
| 2 | 10⁴ J (10 kJ) | A car at highway speed | Manipulate qi externally. Basic techniques. |
| 3 | 10⁵ J (100 kJ) | A lightning bolt | Project qi. Multiple techniques. |
| 4 | 10⁶ J (1 MJ) | 1 kg of TNT | Level a small building. Fly short distances. |
| 5 | 10⁷ J (10 MJ) | A thunderstorm cell | Reshape terrain. Domain expansion begins. |
| 6 | 10⁸ J (100 MJ) | An airliner at cruise | City-scale power. Personal domain. |
| 7 | 10⁹ J (1 GJ) | A severe thunderstorm | Province-scale influence. Reality bends to you. |

### Why 10×?

| Property | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| Tier 0→1 is achievable in minutes | The player starts immediately |
| Each tier feels like a new magnitude | The world reacts differently |
| Cross-tier combat is decisive | A Tier 5 cannot lose to a Tier 3 |
| The grind stretches naturally | Mid-tier takes hours; high-tier takes days |

### Breakthrough

```
breakthroughChance = 0.70 × (1 + energySurplus / tierThreshold)
                   × (1 + comprehension / 200)
                   × stabilityModifier
```

You **can** attempt at exactly the threshold (70%). You **should** accumulate surplus first (90%+). Every joule matters.

## Energy Types — Six Sources, One Total

```
TotalEnergy = Qi + Kinetic + Spirit + Faith + Bloodline + Artifact
```

| Path | Primary Energy | Generated By |
|------|---------------|-------------|
| **Qi** | Qi Energy | Absorbed from sun, moon, earth, ambient qi |
| **Body** | Kinetic Energy | Physical training, combat, extreme exposure |
| **Spirit** | Spirit Energy | Death sites, bound spirits, soul projection |
| **Tech** | Artifact Energy | Stored in crafted items, formations, automatons |
| **Faith** | Faith Energy | Followers' belief, karma, shrines, temples |
| **Beast** | Bloodline Energy | Consuming beasts, awakening genes, transformation |

### Hybrid Paths (Tier 5+)

A secondary path contributes energy at 50% efficiency. A Spirit+Body cultivator's total energy = 100% Spirit + 50% Kinetic. The energy types sum normally; the tier is read from the total.

## Tier Sub-Stages

Each tier has 3 sub-stages, matching the energy progress:

| Sub-Stage | Progress Through Tier | Effect |
|-----------|----------------------|--------|
| **Early** | 0–33% | New tier abilities unlocked. Stats increase. |
| **Mid** | 33–66% | Abilities strengthen. New techniques available. |
| **Late** | 66–100% | At peak. Breakthrough to next tier available at 100%. |

Sub-stage transitions are **automatic events** — they fire when the energy threshold is crossed. Full-tier breakthroughs (Tier 3→4) are **choice-based events** with narrative consequences.

## Energy Generation Rates

| Source | Rate at Tier 0 | Scales With | Rate at Tier 7 |
|--------|---------------|-------------|----------------|
| Qi (ambient) | 0.1 J/tick | Realm, location qi density, cultivation speed | ~5,000 J/tick |
| Kinetic (training) | 0.05 J/tick | Training intensity, body stage, gravity | ~8,000 J/tick |
| Spirit (death sites) | 0.01 J/tick (rare) | Veil thickness, bound spirits, spirit stage | ~12,000 J/tick |
| Faith (followers) | 0.001 J/tick per follower | Follower count, karma magnitude | ~50,000 J/tick (millions) |
| Bloodline (beast consumption) | Burst only | Beast tier consumed, bloodline purity | Burst: up to 10¹⁰ J |
| Artifact (crafted) | Stored at creation | Crafting skill, materials, insights | Stored: up to 10¹² J total |

## Energy in Events

Events award energy directly. The scale is the same regardless of path:

| Event Scale | Energy | Example |
|-------------|--------|---------|
| Minor | 10–50 J | Fought a beast, found a herb |
| Moderate | 50–200 J | Resolved a quest step, saved an NPC |
| Major | 200–1,000 J | Story milestone, breakthrough event |
| Epic | 1,000–10,000 J | Path choice, hybridization |
| Legendary | 10,000–100,000 J | Ascension, ancient inheritance |

## What The Player Sees

The UI shows both systems. The energy tier is the underlying number. The realm name is the in-fiction identity.

│                                                          │

│                                                          │

The player identifies as "Jade Body stage" (their path). The tier number lets them gauge power relative to other paths. An NPC Qi cultivator at "Core Formation" is Tier 4 — a peer. An NPC at "Qi Condensation" is Tier 1–2 — far below.

## How The World Uses Tiers

| Context | What's Used |
|---------|------------|
| NPC dialogue | Realm names ("I see you've reached Jade Body, impressive.") |
| Sect rankings | Realm names + path titles |
| Combat balance | Energy tier (Tier 4 vs Tier 3 = decisive advantage) |
| Location gating | Energy tier ("Danger 5 — requires Tier 5+") |
| Event triggers | Energy tier ("This event fires at Tier 3+") |
| Cross-path comparison | Energy tier ("You are roughly equal to a Core Formation cultivator") |

The tier is the **game mechanic**. The realm name is the **story**.
