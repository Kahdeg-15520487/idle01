## Cosmology & Ancient History

### The Dao and the Three Realms

```
                    ┌──────────────────────┐
                    │    HEAVENLY REALM     │
                    │  (Immortals, Celestial│
                    │   Court, Dao Source)  │
                    └──────────┬───────────┘
                               │ Ascension
                    ┌──────────┴───────────┐
                    │    MORTAL REALM      │
                    │  (The Nine Provinces) │
                    │  Cultivators, mortals,│
                    │  spirit beasts       │
                    └──────────┬───────────┘
                               │ Death / Forbidden Arts
                    ┌──────────┴───────────┐
                    │    NETHER REALM       │
                    │  (Ghosts, demons,     │
                    │   vengeful spirits)   │
                    └──────────────────────┘
```

The Dao is the fundamental truth underlying all existence. Cultivators seek to comprehend it. The Heavenly Realm is not a reward — it's a bureaucracy. Immortals serve the Celestial Court. Those who ascend without Heaven's approval become **Banished Immortals**, hunted by both realms.

### The Shattering (10,000 Years Ago)

The last great war between immortals shattered the original continent into the Nine Provinces. The scars remain:

- **The Sundered Sea** — a rift between provinces where space itself is unstable
- **Fallen Immortal corpses** — scattered across the land, their residual qi mutating beasts and creating Forbidden Zones
- **Broken Heavenly Laws** — in some regions, tribulation lightning strikes randomly, cultivation behaves unpredictably

This history explains everything: why ancient inheritances exist, why Forbidden Zones are dangerous, and why the great sects guard their territories jealously.

## The Great Sects

Four major sects dominate the Verdant South. Each has a philosophy, unique techniques, territory, and political stance.

### 1. Cloud Soaring Sect 云翔宗

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Alignment** | Righteous / Orthodox |
| **Philosophy** | Cultivate virtue, protect the weak, ascend with honor |
| **Specialty** | Wind & Thunder techniques, sword arts |
| **Territory** | Cloud Sect mountain, Misty Peaks (shared) |
| **Reputation** | The most respected sect in the South |
| **Symbol** | White crane against azure sky |
| **Unique Tech** | "Nine Heavens Cloud Step" (movement), "Heavenly Thunder Sword" (attack) |

**Internal Factions:**
- **Sword Peak** — Combat-focused, aggressive, believes in active demon-slaying
- **Cloud Pavilion** — Scholarly, focuses on comprehension and Dao study, produces the best formation masters
- **Thunder Hall** — Body cultivators, physically temper themselves with lightning, brash and direct

**Relationships:**
- Allied with: Verdant Lotus Sect (trade), Green Jade City Council
- Hostile to: Shadow Heaven Sect (ideological enemies)
- Neutral: Blood Lotus Sect (distrust but no open war)
- Protects: Azure Cloud Village, surrounding mortals

### 2. Shadow Heaven Sect 影天宗

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Alignment** | Demonic / Unorthodox |
| **Philosophy** | Power is the only truth. The Dao doesn't care about morality |
| **Specialty** | Shadow & poison techniques, assassination arts |
| **Territory** | Black Wind Gorge, underground networks |
| **Reputation** | Feared. Rumored to practice forbidden soul arts |
| **Symbol** | Black serpent coiled around a moon |
| **Unique Tech** | "Void Shadow Step" (movement), "Soul-Severing Venom Palm" (attack) |

**Internal Factions:**
- **Venom Hall** — Poison specialists, alchemy focused on debilitation
- **Shadow Council** — Political manipulators, spies, information brokers
- **Blood Cult** — The extremist wing, actually practices forbidden techniques (the sect leadership publicly denounces them)

**Relationships:**
- Allied with: None openly (secret ties to underworld merchants)
- Hostile to: Cloud Soaring Sect (centuries-old blood feud)
- Neutral: Blood Lotus Sect (uneasy coexistence)
- Exploits: Villages on the fringe, weak rogue cultivators

### 3. Blood Lotus Sect 血莲宗

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Alignment** | Neutral / Survivalist |
| **Philosophy** | The strong endure, the weak perish. We didn't make the rules |
| **Specialty** | Blood & beast techniques, body tempering, spirit beast taming |
| **Territory** | Crimson Desert, Demon Beast Mountain (contested) |
| **Reputation** | Savage but honorable. They keep their word |
| **Symbol** | Crimson lotus blooming on a pool of blood |
| **Unique Tech** | "Blood Lotus Rebirth" (healing), "Beast God Transformation" (buff) |

**Internal Factions:**
- **Beast Tamers** — Bond with spirit beasts, fight alongside them
- **Blood Warriors** — Pure physical cultivation, extreme body tempering
- **Lotus Healers** — The sect's conscience, use blood arts to heal rather than harm

**Relationships:**
- Allied with: None officially (trade with Green Jade City)
- Hostile to: No major sect (but constant skirmishes with Cloud Soaring over Demon Beast Mountain)
- Neutral: Shadow Heaven Sect (they stay out of each other's way)
- Respected by: Independent cultivators, beast tamers

### 4. Verdant Lotus Sect 青莲宗

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **Alignment** | Righteous / Scholarly |
| **Philosophy** | Through knowledge, enlightenment. The Dao is studied, not fought |
| **Specialty** | Wood & water techniques, alchemy, formations, healing |
| **Territory** | Whispering Forest, scattered herb gardens |
| **Reputation** | Pacifists. The best alchemists and healers in the province |
| **Symbol** | Green lotus with an open book |
| **Unique Tech** | "Myriad Herbs Restoration" (healing), "Thousand Vine Binding Formation" (defense) |

**Internal Factions:**
- **Alchemy Pavilion** — Pill refinement, the sect's economic engine
- **Formation Hall** — Defensive arrays, trap formations, barrier arts
- **Scripture Garden** — Lorekeepers, historians, Dao philosophers (smallest faction, most respected)

**Relationships:**
- Allied with: Cloud Soaring Sect (mutual defense pact), Green Jade City (trade)
- Hostile to: None (neutrality is their shield)
- Neutral: All others (they sell pills to everyone, even Shadow Heaven — through intermediaries)
- Respected by: Everyone (nobody wants to lose access to their pills)

## Minor Factions & Powers

### Independent Organizations

| Group | Nature | Influence |
|-------|--------|-----------|
| **Green Jade Merchant Guild** | Controls trade in the province's hub city | High — everyone needs spirit stones |
| **Wandering Elders** | Retired cultivators who roam freely, offering guidance (or tricks) | Unpredictable |
| **Treasure Hunter Clans** | Families who specialize in raiding ancient ruins | Moderate — source of rare items |
| **Beast Hunter Association** | Regulates spirit beast hunting, issues bounties | Moderate in frontier zones |
| **Heavenly Court Inspectors** | Immortals who occasionally descend to enforce Heavenly Law | Absolute (but rarely seen) |

### Rogue Cultivators

Not everyone belongs to a sect. Rogue cultivators:
- Have no sect protection (easier prey)
- Can learn techniques from any source (no restrictions)
- Are looked down on by sect cultivators ("rootless weeds")
- Sometimes form loose alliances or gangs
- Can be recruited by sects if they show talent
- **The player may choose to remain a rogue cultivator, gaining unique perks and challenges**

### The Underworld

A loose network of black markets, information brokers, and forbidden technique dealers operating in the shadows of every city:
- **The Silent Exchange** — Black market in Green Jade City's sewers
- **Soul Peddlers** — Trade in captured beast souls, spirit remnants, and... other things
- **The Faceless** — Anonymous assassins for hire (rumored Shadow Heaven front)

### Political Events That Affect Gameplay

These are not just flavor — they change encounter tables, merchant availability, and safe zones:

| Event | Trigger | Effect |
|-------|---------|--------|
| **Sect Tournament** | Every 100,000 ticks (~28 hours) | Cloud Sect hosts. Players with high realm can compete. Win = rare technique + reputation |
| **Beast Tide** | Random, more likely in full-moon periods | Demon Beast Mountain overflows. All adjacent locations get combat encounters. Merchants pay premium for beast cores |
| **Shadow Raids** | When player's karma is high near Shadow territory | Shadow Heaven cultivators ambush righteous players. If player is demonic-aligned, they offer alliance |
| **Pill Famine** | Random economic event | Verdant Lotus's latest harvest failed. Pill prices triple. Treasure hunters seek rare herbs for profit |
| **Heavenly Decree** | When player attempts Immortal Ascension | The Celestial Court sends an inspector. Too much "demonic" karma = denied ascension, must cleanse first |
| **Sect Civil War** | Internal faction power struggle (rare) | A sect splits temporarily. Players aligned with that sect must choose a side (or flee). Unique loot from both factions |

### Reputation System

The player accumulates reputation with each sect. This is NOT just karma — it's per-faction standing:

```typescript
interface SectReputation {
  sectId: string;
  standing: number;         // -100 (hated) to +100 (revered)
  rank: SectRank;           // outsider → guest → outer disciple → inner disciple → core disciple → elder
  contributions: number;    // Sect contribution points (earned via missions)
}
```

**Rank Effects:**
| Rank | Benefits |
|------|----------|
| **Outsider** (default) | No special access. Can enter sect grounds if not hostile |
| **Guest** | Can use basic services (merchant, inn) |
| **Outer Disciple** | Access to sect techniques (limited), cultivation spots, sect missions |
| **Inner Disciple** | Better techniques, personal quarters, elder guidance (stat boosts) |
| **Core Disciple** | Signature techniques, political influence, protection from rival sects |
| **Elder** | Sect resources (spirit stone stipend), command NPC disciples, vote on sect matters |

**The player can belong to ONE sect at a time.** Joining a sect that's hostile to your current one triggers defection consequences (bounty, assassins).

## Player Origins

At character creation, the player chooses their origin. This replaces the generic "start in village with nothing" — each origin gives a different starting experience:

### 1. Village Orphan (Classic)

> *"You grew up in Azure Cloud Village, sweeping the temple steps and dreaming of immortals. When an old cultivator passed through and tested your spirit roots, he said you had... potential. Barely."*

| Starting Bonus | Starting Penalty | Starting Location |
|----------------|------------------|-------------------|
| +3 comprehension (curious mind) | -100 spirit stones (poverty) | Azure Cloud Village |
| A rusty iron sword (common weapon) | Low starting luck | |
| Known by villagers (discounts at inn) | No sect affiliation | |

**Narrative hooks:** The old cultivator left a mysterious jade pendant. Is it a key? A tracking device? A legacy?

---

### 2. Disgraced Outer Disciple

> *"You were an outer disciple of the Cloud Soaring Sect. Framed for stealing a technique scroll by a jealous rival. Expelled in disgrace. You will prove your innocence — or surpass them all."*

| Starting Bonus | Starting Penalty | Starting Location |
|----------------|------------------|-------------------|
| +1 sub-stage in Qi Condensation (already started) | -50 Cloud Sect reputation (branded a thief) | Green Jade City (exiled there) |
| Know one basic Cloud Sect technique | Cloud Sect encounters are hostile until reputation recovered | |
| Comprehension +5 (sect training) | Cannot join Cloud Sect until quest completed | |

**Narrative hooks:** Who framed you? The rival is now inner disciple. A revenge arc or redemption arc.

---

### 3. Merchant's Heir

> *"Your family runs a modest trade caravan. You've seen the world — and you've seen what cultivators can do. Why haggle over silks when you could seize the heavens?"*

| Starting Bonus | Starting Penalty | Starting Location |
|----------------|------------------|-------------------|
| +500 spirit stones (family wealth) | -2 all combat stats (soft life) | Green Jade City |
| 10% discount at all merchants (haggling skill) | -3 comprehension (not book-smart) | |
| Random uncommon treasure in inventory | Rogue cultivators see you as easy prey | |

**Narrative hooks:** Family connections in every city. A rival merchant house may have... darker backers.

---

### 4. Wanderer from Another Province

> *"You fled the Northern Wastes when the Ice Phoenix Sect purged your clan. The South is strange and warm, but the Dao is the same everywhere. You will rebuild."*

| Starting Bonus | Starting Penalty | Starting Location |
|----------------|------------------|-------------------|
| Starts at a **random border location** (variety!) | All NPCs treat you as foreign (-10 initial reputation with all sects) | Random: Whispering Forest, Shattered Coast, or Crimson Desert |
| +5 luck (survived impossible odds) | Cannot read local script initially (some technique scrolls require comprehension check) | |
| Unique foreign technique (not learnable elsewhere) | No local knowledge (world map partially hidden) | |

**Narrative hooks:** Are Ice Phoenix Sect agents hunting you? Does your clan have allies in the South?

---

### 5. Banished Immortal's Bloodline

> *"Your ancestor was an immortal who defied the Celestial Court. The bloodline is cursed — tribulations strike harder, breakthroughs hurt more. But the blood of an immortal flows in your veins."*

| Starting Bonus | Starting Penalty | Starting Location |
|----------------|------------------|-------------------|
| +50% Qi capacity (denser dantian) | Tribulation chance +30% (Heaven hates your bloodline) | Random remote location (hiding) |
| Start with a fragment of an immortal-rank technique (sealed, unseals with realm) | Heavenly Court Inspectors may hunt you (rare but deadly encounter) | |
| +10 attack (immortal physique remnant) | -5 karma (sins of the ancestor) | |

**Narrative hooks:** What did your ancestor do? The technique fragment holds memories. Unsealing it reveals the truth.

---

### 6. Spirit Beast Bonded

> *"You were lost in the Whispering Forest as a child. A spirit beast found you. Raised you. You understand the wild better than human tongues."*

| Starting Bonus | Starting Penalty | Starting Location |
|----------------|------------------|-------------------|
| +1 companion spirit beast (fights alongside you, grows with you) | -5 comprehension (raised by beasts, not scholars) | Deep Whispering Forest |
| +10 speed, +5 instinct (wild survival) | -10 initial sect reputation (seen as a wildling) | |
| Beast encounters 50% more likely to be friendly | Cannot read technique scrolls initially | |

**Narrative hooks:** Your beast companion has a mysterious origin. Sects want to capture it. You want to protect it.

## How World Building Drives Gameplay

### 1. Encounter Tables Change Based on Faction Standing

```
Location: Misty Peaks
- Default encounters: beast, treasure, peaceful
- If Cloud Sect ally: "Sect Patrol" encounter (+reputation, free healing)
- If Shadow Sect hostile: "Shadow Ambush" encounter added to table
- If Blood Lotus neutral: they ignore you
- If Verdant Lotus ally: "Herb Gathering" encounter (free pills)
```

### 2. Available Services Change

```
Location: Green Jade City
- Merchant Guild: always available
- Cloud Sect Embassy: only if Cloud Sect standing > -20
- Shadow Sect Underground: only if Shadow Sect standing > -30 (hidden, must discover)
- Sect Technique Exchange: only for inner disciples+
```

### 3. Travel Safety Changes

```
Traveling through: Demon Beast Mountain
- Blood Lotus Sect territory: safe passage if standing > 0
- Cloud Soaring hostile: they'll attack on sight here
- Neutral: prepare for combat
```

### 4. Quest Hooks From Reputation

```
- "The Cloud Sect is recruiting for a beast tide defense. Join them?" (+rep, +loot)
- "A Verdant Lotus alchemist needs rare herbs from Forbidden Valley. They'll reward with rare pills."
- "Shadow Heaven offers you a contract: eliminate a rival cultivator." (+rep with Shadow, -karma, Cloud becomes hostile)
```

### 5. The Player's Identity Shapes the Story

Your origin + sect choice + karma level + reputation create an emergent identity:

- Village orphan who joined the Cloud Sect → **classic hero**
- Merchant heir turned rogue cultivator → **opportunist**
- Banished bloodline who allied with Shadow Heaven → **tragic villain**
- Spirit beast bonded who joined Blood Lotus → **wild card**

These aren't just cosmetic — they change available techniques, NPC reactions, encounter frequency, and even the victory condition (a demonic cultivator's "ascension" may involve conquering the Nether Realm instead of the Heavenly Court).
