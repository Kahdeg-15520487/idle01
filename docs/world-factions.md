# The World's Power Structures — Sects, Guilds, Pacts & Loners

> **Role**: Define every type of organization a player can interact with, from the six first-rate sects to the hermit who wants to be left alone.

---

## The Hierarchy of Power

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                    FIRST-RATE SECTS                          │
│  6 total — one per cultivation path. Controls territory.    │

├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                    SECOND-RATE SECTS                        │

├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                    THIRD-RATE SECTS & CLANS                 │
│  ~20 per province. Controls a village, training ground,    │

│  who left a higher sect.                                   │
├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│                    GUILDS & ASSOCIATIONS                     │
```

## The Six First-Rate Sects

Each cultivation path has exactly one **first-rate sect** — the organization that represents that path's highest concentration of power, knowledge, and tradition. Not all cultivators of a path join their first-rate sect, but the sect shapes how that path is perceived by the world.

| Path | First-Rate Sect | Territory | Tier 5+ Members | Philosophy |
|------|----------------|-----------|-----------------|------------|
| **Qi** | Cloud Soaring Sect 云翔宗 | Misty Peaks + 3 cities | 9 | Righteous cultivation. Balance the Dao. Protect the weak. |
| **Body** | Iron Mountain Sect 铁山宗 | Blackstone Range + The Crater | 6 | The flesh is the ultimate truth. Break everything that breaks. |
| **Spirit** | Shadow Heaven Sect 影天宗 | Black Wind Gorge + Nether Rift | 7 | Power is the only truth. The Dao does not care about morality. |
| **Tech** | Eternal Mechanism Pavilion 永机阁 | Green Jade City (HQ) + 2 workshops | 5 | The universe is a machine. Learn its gears. Build better ones. |
| **Faith** | Temple of Ten Thousand Lights 万光寺 | Golden Summit (capital of a theocratic nation) | 4 | Enlightenment through devotion. Serve the people. They will save you. |
| **Beast** | Blood Lotus Sect 血莲宗 | Crimson Desert + Demon Beast Mountain | 6 | The wild does not judge. Accept your blood. Evolve or die. |

---

### 1. Cloud Soaring Sect 云翔宗 — Qi Path

*"Cultivate virtue. Protect the weak. Ascend with honor."*

**Territory**: Cloud Soaring Mountain (HQ), Azure Cloud Village (protectorate), three secondary cities.
**Members**: ~2,000 disciples, 9 elders (Tier 5+), 1 ancestor (Tier 6, name: Elder Azure Cloud, age: 3,000 years, rarely appears).
**Relationships**:
- Allied with: Eternal Mechanism Pavilion (tech trade), Temple of Ten Thousand Lights (shared moral values)
- Hostile to: Shadow Heaven Sect (blood feud, 500 years running)
- Neutral: Iron Mountain Sect (respect but distance), Blood Lotus Sect (uneasy coexistence)
- Provides missions to: ~6 second-rate and 15 third-rate sects under its protection.

A player who joins Cloud Soaring gets: access to qi cultivation techniques, protection from demonic cultivators, sect cultivation spots (+200% speed), and a target on their back from Shadow Heaven.

---

### 2. Iron Mountain Sect 铁山宗 — Body Path

*"The body does not lie. The body does not negotiate. The body endures."*

**Territory**: Blackstone Range (a mountain range of black iron ore), The Crater (a meteor impact site used for gravity training).
**Members**: ~500 disciples (smallest first-rate sect — body cultivation is brutal and not everyone survives), 6 elders, 0 ancestors (body cultivators rarely reach Tier 6 because their bodies deteriorate before they can transcend).

**Culture**: The Iron Mountain Sect has no written techniques. Everything is taught by doing, by feeling, by being hit until you understand. New disciples spend their first year being beaten by elders. Those who survive earn the right to train. Those who thrive become unbreakable.

**Relationships**:
- Allied with: Blood Lotus Sect (body + beast = primal kinship)
- Friendly with: Cloud Soaring Sect (respect for discipline)
- Neutral: Eternal Mechanism Pavilion (mutual incomprehension — "why craft armor when you can grow it?")
- Hostile to: Shadow Heaven Sect (they tried to turn body cultivators into undead soldiers. It did not go well.)
- Distant: Temple of Ten Thousand Lights ("Faith won't save you from a broken spine.")

A player who joins Iron Mountain gets: unarmed combat mastery, extreme training grounds, permanent HP bonuses from surviving the initiation, and a body that slowly becomes less human.

---

### 3. Shadow Heaven Sect 影天宗 — Spirit Path

*"Power is the only truth. The Dao does not care about morality. Neither do we."*

**Territory**: Black Wind Gorge (HQ), Nether Rift (a location where the Spirit Realm bleeds into the physical), underground networks beneath three cities.
**Members**: ~1,500 disciples, 7 elders, 1 ancestor (a wraith-like Tier 6 who may or may not still be alive — no one has seen their face in 200 years).

**Culture**: Shadow Heaven does not recruit openly. They find you. A disciple of Shadow Heaven is expected to advance by any means necessary — betrayal, assassination, forbidden arts — as long as the sect benefits. Internal betrayal is also expected. The sect ranks are a Darwinian hierarchy. Only the paranoid survive.

**Relationships**:
- Allied with: No official allies. Secret trade with Eternal Mechanism Pavilion (they buy artifacts, they pay well, no questions asked).
- Hostile to: Cloud Soaring Sect (blood feud), Temple of Ten Thousand Lights (faith is antithetical to their philosophy)
- Exploits: Iron Mountain Sect territory (body cultivator corpses are valuable for necromantic experiments)

A player who joins Shadow Heaven gets: access to forbidden soul arts, bound spirit armies, assassination missions, and the constant paranoia that their own sect brothers are plotting against them. Karma is a resource here — negative karma unlocks techniques.

---

### 4. Eternal Mechanism Pavilion 永机阁 — Tech Path

*"The universe is a machine. Study it. Improve it. The Creator is not offended by modifications."*

**Territory**: Green Jade City (HQ — the pavilion's workshops occupy an entire district), two satellite workshops in Misty Peaks and the Crimson Desert border.
**Members**: ~800 disciples, 5 elders, 2 ancestors (both World Smiths who live in pocket dimensions accessible from the HQ).

**Culture**: The Eternal Mechanism Pavilion is the least sect-like of the first-rate sects. There are no robes, no rituals, no hierarchy based on combat power. Disciples are ranked by crafting skill and the complexity of their creations. An Apprentice (Tier 1) can become a Master Artificer (Tier 3) much faster than a Qi cultivator can reach Core Formation — but the Pavilion's combat power outside of prepared war machines is weak. They need allies.

**Relationships**:
- Allied with: Cloud Soaring Sect (defense pact — Cloud Soaring protects Pavilion workshops, Pavilion equips Cloud Soaring disciples)
- Trade with: Everyone. The Pavilion sells to all sects, including Shadow Heaven (through intermediaries). They are the economic engine of the province.
- Neutral: Iron Mountain Sect (they buy each other's products — Iron Mountain supplies raw ore, Pavilion supplies forged weapons)
- Tense: Temple of Ten Thousand Lights (the Temple dislikes that the Pavilion arms everyone, including demonic cultivators. The Pavilion argues that knowledge cannot be unmade. They are both right.)

A player who joins the Pavilion gets: access to schematics, crafting bonuses, automaton assistants, and a steady income. They are also expected to share their inventions with the sect — nothing is truly private here.

---

### 5. Temple of Ten Thousand Lights 万光寺 — Faith Path

*"The light does not discriminate. The light does not ask where you have been. It simply illuminates the way forward."*

**Territory**: Golden Summit (HQ — a city-temple complex that is also the capital of a small theocratic nation), satellite monasteries across three provinces.
**Members**: ~3,000 monks and nuns, 4 elders (High Priests), 1 ancestor (a Living Buddha who has not spoken in 400 years but whose mere presence suppresses beast tides across the entire region).

**Culture**: The Temple does not recruit — aspirants must walk to Golden Summit on a pilgrimage that can take years. Many die on the way. Those who arrive are accepted regardless of past crimes, past paths, or past failures. The Temple is famous for taking in defectors from Shadow Heaven and protecting them. This has caused... tensions.

Faith cultivation is slow and requires community. The Temple provides that community — large enough to generate massive faith energy, disciplined enough to direct it.

**Relationships**:
- Allied with: Cloud Soaring Sect (shared moral values), Iron Mountain Sect (body cultivators and monks respect each other's discipline)
- Hostile to: Shadow Heaven Sect (the Temple protects defectors from Shadow Heaven. Shadow Heaven considers this a declaration of war.)
- Uneasy: Eternal Mechanism Pavilion ("Your machines do not pray. Are you sure they are alive?")
- Distant: Blood Lotus Sect (the Temple does not condemn beast cultivation, but it does not embrace it either)

A player who joins the Temple gets: faith cultivation support, sanctuary from enemies, healing services, and a moral framework that affects which dialogue options are available (certain choices are simply not allowed for Temple members).

---

### 6. Blood Lotus Sect 血莲宗 — Beast Path

*"The wild does not judge. Accept your blood. Evolve or die."*

**Territory**: Crimson Desert (HQ — a harsh wasteland where only the strong survive), Demon Beast Mountain (contested with wild beasts, used as training grounds).
**Members**: ~1,200 disciples, 6 elders, 0 ancestors (beast cultivators rarely reach Tier 6 because society hunts them before they get that far. The current leaders are determined to change this.)

**Culture**: The Blood Lotus Sect is the only first-rate sect that accepts **anyone** — beast cultivators, rogue cultivators, exiles from other sects, escaped slaves, monster-human hybrids, people with no other place to go. This makes them the most diverse and the most volatile sect. Internal fights are common. Murder is frowned upon but not uncommon. The sect leadership stays in power by being stronger than anyone who challenges them.

The sect's name comes from their signature technique: a lotus-shaped blood formation that can absorb the life force of anything within it — beast, cultivator, or mortal — and redistribute it to sect members. Ethical? No. Effective? Yes.

**Relationships**:
- Allied with: Iron Mountain Sect (body cultivators and beast cultivators share a primal understanding)
- Trade with: Eternal Mechanism Pavilion (they buy weapons. They pay in rare beast parts.)
- Hostile to: Cloud Soaring Sect (ideological — Cloud Soaring sees beast cultivation as an abomination), Shadow Heaven Sect (Shadow Heaven tried to enslave their members. Blood Lotus does not forgive.)
- Neutral: Temple of Ten Thousand Lights (the Temple has never attacked them. This is the highest form of respect Blood Lotus expects.)

A player who joins Blood Lotus gets: acceptance regardless of race or past, access to beast cores for consumption, transformation training, and protection from the rest of the world. The price? You fight for the sect when called. And you never betray the blood pact.

## Second-Rate Sects — The Alternative Paths

Below the first-rate sects sit organizations that are not weaker — they are **different interpretations** of the same path. Some are breakaways founded by defectors. Some are heresies that the first-rate sect condemns. Some are experiments in a direction the first-rate sect refuses to explore.

### Six Alternative-Path Sects

| Path | First-Rate (Orthodox) | Second-Rate (Alternative) | Core Difference |
|------|----------------------|--------------------------|-----------------|
| **Qi** | Cloud Soaring — store qi in dantian, balance with nature | **Greedy Furnace Society** 贪炉会 — store qi in EVERY organ, not just dantian | Orthodox cultivates slowly, safely. These cultivators pack qi into their liver, lungs, spleen, even bone marrow. Result: explosive bursts of power in short fights. Consequence: organs rupture, lifespan -50%, constant pain. They are battlefield monsters who die young. Cloud Soaring officially denounces them. Shadow Heaven hires them as mercenaries. |
| **Body** | Iron Mountain — temper the body to endure everything | **Flesh Sculptors** 塑肉匠 — surgically modify and graft flesh for power | Orthodox endures, until the body becomes unbreakable. These cultivators **change** the body — graft beast muscle onto human skeleton, weave metal threads into tendons, implant qi crystals under skin. They look monstrous. They ARE monstrous. Iron Mountain considers them abominations who pervert the sacred unity of the flesh. Blood Lotus trades with them (beast parts for experiments). |
| **Spirit** | Shadow Heaven — command the dead, dominate spirits | **Verdant Soul Order** 青魂会 — heal the living, guide the dead peacefully | Founded by a defector from Shadow Heaven who saw what the sect did to innocent souls and walked away. This order heals, mends, and helps souls pass on. They are the only spirit cultivators welcomed in cities. Shadow Heaven hunts them as traitors. Cloud Soaring protects them as allies. Their techniques are weaker in combat but stronger in karma. They run free clinics in every slum. |
| **Tech** | Eternal Mechanism — build machines, study natural laws | **Spark of Ruin** 灭星火 — build only weapons, destroy everything | While the Pavilion builds for every purpose, the Spark of Ruin builds exclusively for destruction. They do not see a problem with this. "A sword is a tool. A bomb is a tool. Morality is a tool. We build tools." The Pavilion officially expelled them 80 years ago for selling a weapon that leveled a city. They now operate underground, selling to Shadow Heaven and anyone else who pays. Their creations are devastating, unstable, and brilliant. |
| **Faith** | Temple of Ten Thousand Lights — faith through karma, Buddhist themes | **Celestial Chantry** 天唱者 — faith through prayer, Abrahamic themes | The Temple teaches that faith grows through deeds and karma — what you DO shapes your connection to the divine. The Chantry teaches that faith is inherent — you are saved or damned by the content of your belief, not your actions. A mortal who believes purely but has never done a good deed is closer to God than a cultivator who does good deeds but doubts. The two faiths are in cold war. Both claim the other misunderstands divinity. Pilgrims sometimes switch sides and are never heard from again. |
| **Beast** | Blood Lotus — consume any beast, mix bloodlines freely | **Pureclaw Circle** 纯爪会 — follow one bloodline to its purest extreme | Blood Lotus accepts chaos — consume everything, evolve randomly, see what sticks. Pureclaw rejects this. A follower of Pureclaw chooses ONE bloodline at the start of their path and consumes ONLY from that lineage for life. A Pureclaw wolf cultivator drinks wolf blood, eats wolf hearts, sleeps with wolves. The result is a bloodline purity that Blood Lotus cannot match — but also a narrowness that makes them predictable. Pureclaw cultivators at Tier 4 are stronger than Blood Lotus at Tier 4 — but Blood Lotus has six Tier 5s and Pureclaw has one. They were once part of Blood Lotus. A schism over methodology split them 200 years ago. |

### Affiliated Second-Rate Sects

These are smaller, conventional second-rate sects that owe allegiance to a first-rate power or operate independently. They are grouped by theme.

#### Blood & Vampiric Sects

These sects revolve around consuming blood — spirit, beast, or mortal — to gain power. They are universally feared and rarely trusted.

| Name | Path | Affiliation | Territory | Leader | Notable For |
|------|------|------------|-----------|--------|-------------|
| **Crimson Lotus Vampire Clan** 赤莲血族 | Spirit/Beast hybrid | Shadow Heaven (patron) | Subterranean catacombs beneath Misty Peaks | Tier 5 elder vampire (3,000+ years old) | Drink cultivators' blood to steal their shén and memories. Each generation of vampire is weaker than the last, so the eldest are godlike. They can convert mortals into lesser vampires. Shadow Heaven uses them as spies and assassins — a vampire can live inside a city for decades, unnoticed, waiting. |
| **Blooddrinker Coven** 饮血盟 | Spirit | Independent (no sect alliance) | Wandering between villages near Black Wind Gorge | Tier 4 | Less refined than the Crimson Lotus. These are cultivators who turned to blood consumption out of desperation — failed qi cultivators who could not absorb ambient energy efficiently. They drink beast blood for survival, mortal blood for strength, and cultivator blood for power. They are hunted by everyone. They survive by never staying in one place. | **Ancestor Veneration Society** 祭祖会 | Spirit | Neutral (trade with Temple of Ten Thousand Lights) | Mountain tomb complex | Tier 4 | Do not drink living blood. They preserve and consume the blood of their own ancestors, believing that a family's power accumulates across generations in the bloodline. Every generation adds their blood to a communal reservoir. The current generation drinks from it. They are not hunted because they do not kill — they simply wait for their elders to die naturally, then consume. Disturbing to outsiders, but technically ethical. |

| **Sundered Bow Order** 断弓门 | Qi (archery) | Independent (neutral) | The Great Ridge, a valley with mile-long sightlines | Tier 4 | Bow cultivators who believe the arrow is the purest expression of intent. ### Joining an Alternative-Path Sect

Alternative-path sects have **lower entry requirements** than first-rate sects (less reputation needed, no alignment checks) but come with **social consequences**:

| Sect | Social Penalty | Mechanical Tradeoff |
|------|---------------|-------------------|
| Greedy Furnace Society | -30 reputation with Cloud Soaring | +50% burst damage per fight, -40% max HP |
| Flesh Sculptors | -40 reputation with Iron Mountain | Can graft beast parts for temporary stat boosts, permanent humanity loss |
| Verdant Soul Order | -60 reputation with Shadow Heaven (treaty) | +50% healing effectiveness, -30% combat damage |
| Spark of Ruin | -40 reputation with Eternal Mechanism | Crafted weapons deal +50% damage, 20% chance to backfire |
| Celestial Chantry | -30 reputation with Temple of Ten Thousand Lights | Faith generation +50% through prayer (no deeds needed), karma locked (cannot change after joining) |
| Pureclaw Circle | -20 reputation with Blood Lotus | Single-bloodline bonus +100%, locked into one beast type permanently |

A player who joins an alternative-path sect is making a statement. Their path is valid — but the world will not let them forget it.

## Guilds & Associations

Sect-neutral organizations that accept members from any path or none.

### Merchant Guild 商盟
- HQ: Green Jade City
- Branches: Every city and most large villages
- Membership: Open to anyone with spirit stones to trade
- Services: Buy/sell items, transfer goods between cities, loans, information on pricing
- Player benefit: Discount on goods, access to restricted trade items, ability to sell remotely
- Does not care about your karma, your path, or your past. Only your stones.

### Adventurer's Association 游历者协会
- HQ: None (mobile council of five Tier 4 cultivators)
- Branches: Every major location has a bulletin board
- Membership: Prove you can complete a contract (varies by rank)
- Services: Bounty board, group formation, quest contracts, wilderness guides
- Player benefit: Steady income, meeting other cultivators, access to dangerous locations with support
- Ranks: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Legendary (based on completed contracts)

### Beast Hunter Union 猎兽公会
- HQ: Border between Misty Peaks and Demon Beast Mountain
- Branches: All dangerous locations
- Membership: Must pass a combat trial against a Tier 3 beast
- Services: Beast tracking, carcass processing, core extraction, bounty on specific monsters
- Player benefit: Discount on beast-core refinement, access to hunting parties, shared maps
- Tensions: Does not accept beast cultivators as members (hunters and beasts do not mix)

### Alchemist's Circle 丹师会
- HQ: Hidden (revealed only to members)
- Branches: Invitation-only, present in most cities
- Membership: Must demonstrate skill — refine a pill of at least Rare quality
- Services: Recipe exchange, material trade, pill appraisal, cauldron rental
- Player benefit: Learn rare recipes, sell pills without merchant markup, alchemy skill bonuses
- Culture: Secretive, hierarchical, obsessed with purity of ingredients

### Wanderer's Pact 散修盟
- HQ: None
- Branches: Signal fires at agreed locations across the province
- Membership: Any rogue cultivator (non-sect) who swears the pact's oath
- Services: Mutual protection, resource sharing, safe houses, information network
- Player benefit: Rogue cultivators have allies despite being sectless
- Limitation: The Pact has no central authority. It can't defend you against a first-rate sect. But it can hide you.

## Alliances & Pacts

Not organizations — **agreements** that can be formed between players and NPC factions or between NPCs that affect the world state.

### Types

| Type | Duration | Example |
|------|----------|---------|
| **Military Alliance** | 5,000 ticks (~1.5 hours) or until objective achieved | Cloud Soaring + Eternal Mechanism Pavilion vs Beast Tide |
| **Non-Aggression Pact** | 10,000 ticks (~3 hours) | Blood Lotus + Iron Mountain agree not to raid each other |
| **Trade Agreement** | Permanent until broken | Eternal Mechanism Pavilion sells weapons to everyone equally |
| **Blood Pact** | Lifetime (individuals) or 50,000 ticks (sects) | Two players (or a player and an NPC) swear to protect each other |
| **Marriage Alliance** | Permanent | Two sect heirs marry to seal a political alliance |
| **Tribute Relationship** | Ongoing | A second-rate sect pays 10% of its income to Cloud Soaring for protection |

### How the Player Interacts

The player can:
- **Propose alliances** (if they have rank to speak for their sect)
- **Accept pacts** from NPCs (a rogue cultivator asks to form a blood pact)
- **Break agreements** (consequences: reputation loss, war, assassins)
- **Discover existing pacts** (event reveals: "Cloud Soaring and Eternal Mechanism just renewed their defense pact — the balance of power shifts")

## Legendary Loners

Individuals so powerful they have no sect. Players can seek them out, be targeted by them, or (at high enough tiers) challenge them.

### The Hermit of the Forbidden Valley 禁谷隐士
- **Path**: Qi (Dao Seeking, Tier 7)
- **Location**: Forbidden Valley (deepest part)
- **Known for**: Has not spoken in 800 years. Answers no summons. Fought off a Shadow Heaven invasion of the Valley single-handedly in year 421 and returned to meditation immediately after.
- **Player interaction**: May appear as a vision during meditation if player's Dao comprehension is high enough. Grants insights.
- **Rumor**: He is not a person. He is a technique that became self-aware.

### The Walking Mountain 山行者
- **Path**: Body (Indestructible Flesh, Tier 6)
- **Location**: Wandering. Last seen heading toward the Volcanic Caldera.
- **Known for**: Is seven feet tall, has not worn a shirt in recorded history, and once caught a collapsing cliff on his shoulders and held it for three days until everyone below escaped.
- **Player interaction**: May challenge the player to a test of strength. If the player impresses him, he teaches a technique. If the player insults him, they get thrown.
- **Rumor**: His body is so dense that gravity around him is distorted. Grass grows sideways near him.

### The Widow of Black Wind 黑风寡妇
- **Path**: Spirit (Soul Dominion, Tier 5)
- **Location**: Black Wind Gorge (a specific haunted tower)
- **Known for**: Her husband was killed by Cloud Soaring Sect in a raid. She consumed her own grief and became a spirit cultivator. She now commands a legion of 300 bound spirits — all former Cloud Soaring disciples she has personally killed.
- **Player interaction**: Can be an ally (if player is anti-Cloud Soaring), a threat (if player is Cloud Soaring), or a quest giver (she needs help recovering her husband's remains from Cloud Soaring's vault).
- **Warning**: Her spirits include children. She does not discriminate.

### The Last Smith 最后的铁匠
- **Path**: Tech (World Smith, Tier 6)
- **Location**: An invisible pocket dimension. The entrance is a specific anvil in a specific blacksmith shop in Green Jade City. If you don't know what you're looking for, you'll never find it.
- **Known for**: Forged the weapons of three of the six first-rate sect founders. Now retired. Works on small projects for fun. Once made a bird automaton that can sing songs from the Immortal Age.
- **Player interaction**: If the player brings rare materials, the Last Smith may craft a legendary item for them. The Smith does not accept payment. Only materials that tell a story.
- **Rumor**: The Smith is not human. The Smith is the ghost of a human who loved making things so much that death could not stop them.

### The Last Demon King 最后魔王
- **Path**: Faith (Tier 6 — Ascended Deity)
- **Location**: Unknown. His temples have been destroyed. His followers have scattered. He is alone.
- **Known for**: He was once a Demon King with a million followers. He realized that his power came from fear, not faith, and that fear-based power is hollow. He destroyed his own temples, freed his followers, and walked away. He is now a Tier 6 cultivator with zero faith income, surviving on residual belief from people who still remember him with gratitude rather than terror.
- **Player interaction**: A rare encounter that only triggers if the player has karma between -30 and +30 (neutral alignment). He offers a choice: learn a technique that converts despair into power, or help him find a purpose beyond worship.
- **The only being in the world who voluntarily went from Tier 6 to Tier 3 and then climbed back alone.**

### The Great Nothing 大空
- **Path**: None. Or all. No one is sure.
- **Tier**: Unmeasurable.
- **Location**: A small cave in the Azure Cloud Village cliffside. Has always been there. No one notices it unless they are specifically looking for it.
- **Known for**: If you find the cave, there is a figure sitting in the dark. It will answer one question. Not "tell me the Dao." Not "make me immortal." One honest question. It will answer truthfully, briefly, and then you will forget you ever asked. The answer will remain in your actions, not your memory.
- **Player interaction**: The only "quest" related to The Great Nothing is: find the cave before you ascend. It does not give shén. It does not give techniques. It gives something else.

## How The Player Interacts With Each Layer

| Layer | First Contact | Ongoing Interaction | Leaving |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|---------|
| **First-rate sect** | Build rep through missions/events. Join after trial. | Climb ranks, represent sect, access exclusive resources | Rank reset or enmity depending on how you leave |
| **Second-rate sect** | Can join directly (lower requirements) | Specialized training, regional missions | Easy to leave, no hard feelings |
| **Third-rate sect/clan** | Befriend or defeat the leader | Local quests, minor resources, safe houses | Trivial. They need you more than you need them. |
| **Guild** | Pay fee or pass a trial | Unlimited membership (no loyalty required) | No consequences. Just stop showing up. |
| **Alliance/pact** | Negotiate terms (rep-based) | Mutual defense, shared intel, trade | Breaking a pact = reputation loss, possibly war |
| **Legendary loner** | Seek them out (event-triggered) | One-time interaction or rare repeat | They don't care. You're not important enough. |
